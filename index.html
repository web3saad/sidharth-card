About MadeTrue

A marketplace for every maker. An opportunity for every small business.

MADETRUE is a digital marketplace that brings customers closer to the people who make and sell products.

From home-based entrepreneurs and independent makers to artisans, small producers and Self-Help Groups (SHGs), we aim to give small businesses a platform to showcase their products, reach more customers and grow their businesses.

Home-based entrepreneursIndependent makersArtisansSmall producersSelf-Help Groups

We believe great products and talented makers deserve to be discovered, regardless of the size of their business.

Through a simple digital experience, MADETRUE makes product discovery easier for customers while creating new opportunities for the people behind every product.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive marketplace where every maker has an opportunity to be seen, every small business has room to grow, and every customer can discover something meaningful.
